# Farmologic — brand brief

The **brand and identity** side of the brief: who Farmologic is, what the brand platform is, and
which visual and verbal rules are locked. Drawn from the client's own material —
`design-system/uploads/Farmologic-Brand-Presentation.pdf` ("Brand Identity — A Walkthrough",
prepared by Nuvio), the 38 supplied creatives in `design-system/uploads/`, and the brand sheet at
`design-system/assets/brand-sheet.jpeg`.

> **Read [`client-brief.md`](client-brief.md) alongside this, and treat it as authoritative where
> the two disagree.** That document is cleaned from the 4 August 2026 client call and carries the
> things only a conversation could produce: the hard messaging rules (no competitor comparison, no
> "only brand" claim, no unsupported health claims, "purest" not "largest"), the two adulteration
> mechanisms that are the strongest differentiator available, the agreed launch dates, the
> pre-book flow, and the instruction never to publish a harvest volume.
>
> Specifically, `client-brief.md` supersedes this file on **dates** (harvest late November,
> sampling opens 1 December — not the Dec 2026 harvest stated below) and on **every commercial
> figure**.

Anything not confirmed by the deck or the creatives is marked **[assumption]** and listed again
under *Open questions*.

The name is the thesis: **farm + logic**. Growing things well is agriculture. Growing them the
same way every single time is engineering. The brand sits where those two have to agree.

---

## 2. The company

Farmologic grows one mushroom, indoors, in a sealed and instrumented facility in Bengaluru. Not a
field, not a shed. Temperature, humidity, CO₂ and photoperiod are held to set points per phase and
logged continuously; every batch is traced from substrate lot to certificate of analysis.

The company is pre-revenue and does not hide it. First cultivation starts September 2026; the
first harvest lands December 2026. The commercial narrative is built on that fact rather than
around it — *sell the harvest before it is grown*, with allocations agreed in the order they are
confirmed. The scarcity is real, not manufactured.

Strategically, the play is import substitution. India imports most of the cordyceps that ends up
in Indian supplements, from a market where adulteration is a documented problem and price moves
with a season nobody controls. Farmologic replaces that with a domestic, traceable, repeatable
supply.

## 3. What they sell

One species — *Cordyceps militaris*, fruiting body only, never mycelium-on-grain — in three
formats. The three-pack lineup is established in the client's own hero photography
(`design-system/assets/photo-hero-dark.jpeg`):

| Format | Pack shown | Benefit triad on pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cordyceps Powder** | Forest pouch, 100 g, "100% Natural" | ENERGY \| FOCUS \| STAMINA |
| **Cordyceps Capsules** | Ivory pouch, 60 capsules, dietary supplement | ENERGY \| IMMUNITY \| VITALITY |
| **Dried Cordyceps** | Olive pouch, 25 g, "Premium Grade" | Premium grade, whole |

Pricing is **"Price on request"** everywhere — no list, quoted against specification and volume.
Market context supplied by the client: dried cordyceps has recently traded in India at roughly
**₹20,000–36,000 per kg**.

Commercial terms on the site (MOQ 1 kg, 50 g sample, 4–6 week lead time, packing, shelf life) and
the full target specification (cordycepin ≥ 0.5 % w/w, moisture ≤ 8 %, 80 mesh, and the release
testing panel) are **[assumption]** — plausible for indoor *C. militaris*, drafted to make the
site commercially usable, and labelled on-page as targets. They need client sign-off. See
*Open questions*.

---

## 4. Who buys

Not consumers. The buyer is a business that puts cordyceps into a product:

- Supplement and nutraceutical brands
- Contract manufacturers
- Functional-beverage startups
- AYUSH formulators
- Sports-nutrition brands
- Extraction houses **[assumption — inferred from the whole-dried format]**

Inside those companies the reader is procurement, R&D or QA. They are evaluating a supplier, and
they will read the certificate of analysis before they read the copy. That shapes everything: the
site states MOQ, lead time, cordycepin %, COA policy, FSSAI and AYUSH status plainly, in writing,
before anyone has to ask.

---

## 5. The commercial goal

**One conversion: a qualified bulk enquiry or sample request.**

No cart. No pricing page. No newsletter. Every route through the site ends at the same form, and
the form asks the questions a trade desk needs to quote — company, format, indicative annual
volume, target launch, and what they are formulating.

Secondary goal: rank for the evaluation-stage searches a formulator runs before they shortlist a
supplier. That is what the `learn.html` notes exist for.

## 6. Brand platform

**Purpose** — *Harnessing nature through science to create premium wellness solutions for a
better tomorrow.*

**Tagline** — *Nature. Science. Wellness.*

**Box line** — *Goodness Inside. Better You. Outlook.*

**Named brand promise — The Farmologic Standard**
> Every batch. Watched as it grows. Verified in the lab. Certified.

This is a named, repeated device, not a one-off headline. It is treated as the spine of the site:
introduced on the home page, given its own page (`standard.html`), and echoed beside the enquiry
form.

**Supporting devices from the creatives**
- Benefit triad: `ROOTED IN NATURE. | BACKED BY SCIENCE. | BUILT FOR WELLNESS.`
- Quality journey: Mindful Sourcing → Sustainable Farming → Rigorous Testing → Science-Backed
  Validation → Effective Wellness
- Five brand benefit marks: Natural Ingredients, Science Backed, Premium Quality, Sustainable
  Practices, Made for Wellness
- Paired-opposition device lines, second clause in gold:
  *"We don't just follow nature. We honour it — from farm to formula."*
  *"Excellence is grown, not added later."*

**Positioning rule — brand leads, product proves.** Never "cordyceps" alone; always paired with
Farmologic.

## 7. Voice

Calm, assured, premium. Declarative short sentences with full stops. First-person plural "we"
speaking to "you", and promise-framing — *"That's our promise to you."*

- Never hypey. No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- B2B copy is precise: MOQ, lead time, cordycepin %, COA, FSSAI and AYUSH numbers stated plainly.
- Real figures; "Price on request" for pricing.
- Headlines in serif, sentence or title case, with one word highlighted in gold or olive
  ("Why Quality Starts **Before Harvest**").
- Eyebrows and labels in uppercase Montserrat, wide tracking ("HERO PRODUCTS", "BUILT FOR YOUR
  BEST").
- Unicode used sparingly as separators only — thin bar `|` in benefit triads, middot `·` in seals.

## 8. Visual identity

Locked, and not up for reinterpretation.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Palette ratio** | ~60 % ivory / 30 % forest / 7 % sage-olive / 3 % gold |
| **Forest green** | `#1E3D2B` (deep `#132A1D`) |
| **Sage / olive** | `#6B8A5E` / `#7C8B4A` |
| **Ivory** | `#F6F4EE` |
| **Charcoal** | `#1A1A1A` |
| **Gold** | `#C8A24A` |
| **Display type** | Cormorant Garamond 500/600, tight leading ~1.08 — the *Nature* half |
| **Body / UI type** | Montserrat 400/500/600, uppercase labels tracked .22–.32em — the *Science* half |
| **Radii** | 0–2 px editorial, 6 px glass, pill only for seals and icon rings |
| **Backgrounds** | Flat ivory canvas; dark sections `linear-gradient(180deg,#132A1D,#1E3D2B)`. No other gradients, ever. |

**Gold is never a block.** Hairlines, seals, small marks, single highlighted words. That is the
whole permitted use.

Other locked rules: the logo is a fixed image, never redrawn or recoloured, and sits on a cream
tile when it has to appear on dark. Botanicals are line-drawn, decorative, low-contrast and
corner-anchored — copied from supplied imagery, never invented. Icons are thin-stroke line marks
inside 1 px circles. Imagery is warm and naturally lit, cream and green; no cool or blue tones, no
black and white.

**The tension, and how it was resolved.** The client asked for "very futuristic". The supplied
creative library is warm, editorial and botanical. Resolution: keep the palette and typefaces
exactly as locked, and deliver futurism through *interaction and atmosphere* instead — dark
"living lab" sections, glass data cards on dark grounds, a scroll-driven grow timeline, monospace-
feel lab readouts, restrained micro-motion. All of it progressive enhancement; copy exists in the
served HTML and `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured.

---

## 9. Hard constraints

1. **Founder anonymity is absolute.** No founder name, photo or bio anywhere. Quotes are
   attributed to "— FARMOLOGIC". Two supplied assets — `assets/brand-sheet.jpeg` and
   `assets/photo-collateral-kit.jpeg` — contain a business-card mockup bearing a name; neither is
   used anywhere on the site, and the name must never be reproduced.
2. **B2B only, cordyceps only.** No consumer store, no second crop, no catalogue.
3. **Brand leads, product proves.** Never "cordyceps" standing alone.
4. **The palette, the two typefaces and the logo are locked.** Change them in the design system
   and re-export; never in the site.
5. **Gold is never a block colour.**
6. **Honest about stage.** Pre-harvest is stated on the home page. Target specifications are
   labelled as targets until a COA confirms them.

## 11. Gaps and open questions

Blocking before launch:

1. **Specification figures.** Cordycepin ≥ 0.5 % w/w, adenosine, moisture ≤ 8 %, ash ≤ 8 %,
   80 mesh, 24-month shelf life, MOQ 1 kg, 50 g sample, 4–6 week lead time, and every grow set
   point on `standard.html`. All **[assumption]**. Confirm or replace.
2. **Trade email.** The site uses `trade@farmologic.com` throughout. Confirm it exists.
3. **Enquiry endpoint.** Both forms fall back to `mailto:` until `data-endpoint` is pointed at a
   real POST target (Formspree, Netlify Forms, CRM webhook).
4. **FSSAI and AYUSH numbers.** Currently stated as "on request".

Non-blocking but worth chasing:

5. **Original icon SVGs** (`~/Farmologic/04-icons/`) — Lucide-style substitutes are in place, as
   the design system sanctioned.
6. **A mark-only / favicon export** — the current favicon is cropped from the locked lockup.
7. **Facility photography.** Only one clean product photograph exists. A grow-room shoot would
   materially strengthen the Standard and grow-timeline sections; everything else supplied is a
   poster with text baked in.
8. **Analytics, privacy policy, consent.** Nothing is loaded and nothing is tracked today.
9. **Testing laboratory.** The site says "independent NABL-accredited". Confirm the partner.
