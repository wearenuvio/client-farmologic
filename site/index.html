The Farmologic Standard

Watched as it grows.
Verified by independent laboratory testing.

  1. Strain & substrate

Inside the grow

From spore to sealed batch.

Wild-collected material varies by altitude, season and collector. Indoors, the variables are ours.

  1. 01
    Inoculation

    Spore to substrate

    Liquid culture from one documented strain, introduced to sterilised substrate in still air. Every jar labelled to its batch before it leaves the bench.

  2. 02
    Colonisation

    The mycelium takes hold

    White mycelium runs through the substrate in the dark. Jars inspected on a fixed schedule; anything contaminated is pulled and recorded, never blended back in.

  3. 03
    Fruiting

    Colour under light

    Light, fresh air and a temperature drop trigger the orange fruiting bodies. Conditions held tightly and logged.

  4. 04
    Harvest & verify

    Cut, dried, verified

    Harvest at defined maturity, dried at low temperature, then a representative sample to an independent laboratory. No batch is released until its result is back and inside specification.

Traceability

Where it came from is the hard part.

Cordyceps reaches buyers through long supply chains where traceability is hard to establish. A specification tells you what material should contain — not where it was grown, or how.

That gap is what we are built around: one facility, one strain, and a written record from substrate to specification. The answer to “where did this come from” is a document, not an assurance.

Questions

What buyers ask first

Is this fruiting body or mycelium on grain?

Fruiting body only — never mycelium-on-grain. The specification states the part used, so your label claim can be accurate.

What cordycepin content can we expect?

We grow to a target and confirm it by HPLC. Content varies with strain and conditions, so the figure that matters is the one measured on your batch.

Do you supply a certificate of analysis?

Yes. Cordycepin and adenosine by HPLC, plus heavy metals, microbiology and pesticide residue — tested independently before release, issued with the batch.

Are you certified?

Not yet. We are a registered company working to a written specification, with applications under way. We name each certification once it is held, and never before.

Can you match a specification we already run?

Often, yes. Send your incoming-material specification and we will tell you plainly whether we can meet it.

What does it cost?

Price on request, against your specification, format and volume — not from a list.
