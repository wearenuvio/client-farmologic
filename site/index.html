The Farmologic Standard

Every batch. Watched as it grows.
Verified in the lab. Certified.

Four things have to be true before a kilogram leaves Bengaluru. We named them so you can hold us to them.

  1. 01 · Strain & substrate

    A single, documented C. militaris strain. Grain and supplement inputs are traced to lot, and every substrate batch is recorded before inoculation.

  2. 02 · Controlled grow

    Temperature, humidity, CO₂ and light are held to set points and logged continuously. No field variables. No season. No surprises in the fruiting.

  3. 03 · Harvest & drying

    Cut at defined maturity, dried low and slow to protect the actives, then milled or left whole. Moisture is checked before anything is sealed.

  4. 04 · Lab verification

    Third-party assay for cordycepin and adenosine, plus heavy metals, microbiology and pesticide residue. The certificate of analysis ships with the batch.

Inside the grow

Ten weeks, from spore to sealed batch.

Cordyceps grown outdoors is a lottery — wild-collected material varies by altitude, season and collector. Indoors, the variables are ours. Here is what a Farmologic cycle looks like, and what we hold at each stage.

Cycle summary
Cycle length
~70 days
Environment
Indoor, sealed
Logging
Continuous
  1. 01
    Day 0–3 · Inoculation

    Spore to substrate

    Liquid culture from a single documented strain is introduced to a sterilised grain substrate in a still-air environment. Every jar is labelled to the batch before it leaves the bench.

    TEMP 22 °CRH 70 %LIGHT Dark
  2. 02
    Day 4–21 · Colonisation

    The mycelium takes hold

    White mycelium runs through the substrate in the dark. Jars are inspected on a fixed schedule; anything showing contamination is pulled and recorded, never blended back in.

    TEMP 20–22 °CRH 65–75 %CO₂ High
  3. 03
    Day 22–55 · Fruiting

    Colour under light

    Light, fresh air and a temperature drop trigger the orange fruiting bodies. This is the stage that decides cordycepin content, so the light cycle and CO₂ are held tightly and logged.

    TEMP 18–20 °CRH 80–90 %LIGHT 12 hCO₂ <1000 ppm
  4. 04
    Day 56–70 · Harvest & verify

    Cut, dried, certified

    Harvested at defined maturity, dried at low temperature to target moisture, then sampled for third-party assay. The batch is sealed and released only once the certificate comes back.

    DRY ≤45 °CMOISTURE ≤8 %RELEASE On COA
