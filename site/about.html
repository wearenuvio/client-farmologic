About

Farm and logic.

The name is the whole thesis. Growing things well is agriculture. Growing them the same way every single time is engineering. Farmologic exists at the point where those two disciplines have to agree.

Our purpose

Harnessing nature through science to create premium wellness solutions for a better tomorrow.

We are a business-to-business grower building science-backed ingredients for the next generation of wellness. We are starting with one: Cordyceps militaris, grown in one facility to one written standard. Get that right, and the next ingredient earns the same treatment.

Why cordyceps, why now

One ingredient first, taken seriously

A large share of the cordyceps used in Indian supplements is imported, and much of it arrives with a specification that cannot be traced back to a particular grow, from a market where traceability and consistency can be difficult to establish.

Farmologic was set up to remove that dependency at the source. A controlled indoor facility in Bengaluru, dedicated entirely to Cordyceps militaris, growing to a written specification and verifying material through independent laboratory testing before release.

We chose to start narrow on purpose. A single organism means the whole facility, strain library, substrate recipe, environmental set points, drying curve and testing panel is tuned to one thing. That is what makes a batch repeatable, and repeatability is what a serious formulator builds a product on. It is also the discipline we intend to carry into every ingredient that follows.

We are pre-revenue, and we would rather say that plainly than dress it up. The first grow is under way; allocations from it are being agreed now, with buyers who would rather secure a known supply early than bid for an unknown one later.

What we hold to

Four things we will not trade away

Grown, not sourced

We grow what we sell. The material carrying our name is material we cultivated ourselves, in our own facility.

Verified, not asserted

Independent laboratory testing, batch by batch, with the certificate available on request. If we have not measured it, we do not claim it.

Made in India, properly

A domestic supply chain with a real address and a facility you are welcome to audit.

Honest about stage

We are pre-harvest and we say so on the homepage. Nothing is stated as a result until a certificate confirms it, and no certification is named until it is held.
