About

Farm and logic.

The name is the thesis. Growing things well is agriculture. Growing them the same way every time is engineering. Farmologic exists where those two have to agree.

Meet the founder

Nature, science, and something built with purpose.

I'm Mandeep Mahadevaiah, the founder of Farmologic.

My connection with agriculture began long before Farmologic. I come from a family with generations in farming, and grew up with an appreciation for the land, for patience, and for producing something with care.

My education took me across disciplines: an MS in Psychology from the United States and an MA in Political Science. Those years brought together three things I believe in: nature, science, and purposeful entrepreneurship.

That is where Farmologic began: a company where natural ingredients are not simply marketed, but understood, cultivated and tested.

Why cordyceps, why now

One ingredient first, taken seriously

Cordyceps reaches Indian formulators through long supply chains, often from overseas, where traceability and consistency are hard to establish.

Farmologic removes that dependency at the source: a controlled indoor facility in Bengaluru growing Cordyceps militaris to a written specification, verified by independent laboratory testing before release.

We start narrow on purpose. A single organism lets the facility, strain library, substrate recipe, set points, drying curve and testing panel all be tuned to one thing. That is what makes a batch repeatable, and repeatability is what a formulator builds a product on.

We are pre-revenue, and would rather say so plainly. The first grow is under way and we are talking to buyers now, so both sides plan against the same information.

What we hold to

Four things we will not trade away

Grown, not sourced

We grow what we sell. Material carrying our name is material we cultivated ourselves.

Verified, not asserted

Every batch is tested by an independent laboratory and its certificate issued with it. If we have not measured it, we do not claim it.

Made in India, properly
