Questions

What buyers ask first

Is this fruiting body or mycelium on grain?

Fruiting body, always. Farmologic does not supply mycelium-on-grain material, and the specification states the part used so your label claim can be accurate.

What cordycepin content can we expect?

We grow to a target of ≥ 0.5 % w/w cordycepin, assayed by HPLC. Fruiting-body cordycepin varies with strain and light cycle, which is exactly why the number that matters is the one on your batch COA rather than the one in a brochure.

Can you match a specification we already run?

Often, yes. Send us your incoming-material spec and we will tell you plainly whether we can meet it, at what volume, and what it would change about the grow. If we cannot, we will say so.

When can we take delivery?

First cultivation begins September 2026; first harvest is December 2026. Allocations from that harvest are being agreed now, in the order they are confirmed. Sample quantities are released to qualified buyers ahead of general supply.

What does it cost?

Price on request, against your volume and format. For context, dried cordyceps in the Indian market has recently traded in the ₹20,000–36,000 per kg range; we will quote against your specification rather than a list.
