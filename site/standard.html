What we commit to

The four obligations

The Standard is not a slogan we apply after the fact. It is four commitments that decide whether a batch is allowed to leave the facility at all.

  1. 01 · Strain & substrate

    Know what went in

    A single documented C. militaris strain, maintained in-house and re-isolated on a schedule rather than passaged indefinitely. Grain and supplement inputs are received against specification and traced to lot. Every substrate batch is recorded before inoculation, so any downstream result can be walked back to its inputs.

  2. 02 · Controlled grow

    Hold the conditions, log the proof

    Temperature, relative humidity, CO₂ and photoperiod are held to set points for each phase and logged continuously. Deviations are recorded with the corrective action taken. Contaminated units are pulled, counted and destroyed — never blended into a good batch to make up weight.

  3. 03 · Harvest & drying

    Protect what you grew

    Harvest at defined maturity, not at convenience. Drying is low-temperature and controlled to a moisture target, because heat is the fastest way to lose the actives you paid to grow. Milling, grading and sealing happen against the same batch record.

  4. 04 · Lab verification

    Certify before release

    A representative sample goes to a third-party laboratory for cordycepin and adenosine by HPLC, plus heavy metals, microbiology and pesticide residue. The batch is not released — and cannot be invoiced — until the certificate is back and inside specification.
