What we commit to

The four obligations

Not a slogan applied after the fact — four commitments that decide whether a batch leaves the facility at all.

  1. 01 · Strain & substrate

    Know what went in

    One documented C. militaris strain, maintained in-house and re-isolated on schedule. Inputs received against specification and traced to lot. Every substrate batch recorded before inoculation, so any result can be walked back to its inputs.

  2. 02 · Controlled grow

    Hold the conditions, log the proof

    Temperature, humidity, CO₂ and photoperiod held to set points per phase and logged continuously. Deviations recorded with the corrective action. Contaminated units are pulled, counted and destroyed — never blended into a good batch.

  3. 03 · Harvest & drying

    Protect what you grew

    Harvest at defined maturity, not at convenience. Low-temperature drying to a moisture target. Milling, grading and sealing against the same batch record.

  4. 04 · Lab verification

    Verify before release

    A representative sample goes to an independent laboratory: cordycepin and adenosine by HPLC, plus heavy metals, microbiology and pesticide residue. No batch is released or invoiced until its result is back and inside specification.
